Booster Pumps

Pressure boosting systems that keep water moving reliably—from residential buildings to compact inline installs and intelligent VFD packages.

Residential

Domestic Booster Pumps

Domestic booster pumps maintain consistent outlet pressure across taps, showers, and appliances—especially in multi-floor buildings where supply pressure drops during peak use.

Hydromax packages combine quiet operation, dry-run protection, and easy integration with storage tanks so households and housing complexes get reliable water without frequent manual intervention.

Compact install

Inline Booster Pumps

Inline boosters install directly in the pipeline, saving floor space in mechanical rooms, rooftops, and utility closets where conventional pump skids will not fit.

They are suited for small commercial sites, clubhouses, and retrofit projects that need a pressure lift without major civil changes—delivering stable flow with minimal footprint and straightforward maintenance access.

Smart control

VFD Booster Systems

Variable-frequency drive booster systems match motor speed to real-time demand, reducing energy use and wear compared with fixed-speed operation during low-flow periods.

Ideal for campuses, hospitals, and industrial facilities with fluctuating consumption, VFD packages provide smooth pressure control, soft starts, and monitoring-ready interfaces for modern building management.
