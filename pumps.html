Water Pumps

Reliable pumping for municipal and industrial water systems.

High Pressure Pumps

Hydromax engineers high-pressure pump systems built for demanding municipal, industrial, and process water applications. From triplex plunger and piston units to reciprocating and jetting packages, our solutions deliver stable flow, precise control, and long-term reliability in the field.

Each pump type is selected and configured for your duty point—pressure, flow, fluid properties, and operating environment—so your plant runs efficiently with lower maintenance and downtime.

Triplex Plunger Pumps

High pressure

Overview

Three-plunger positive displacement pumps built for stable, high-pressure flow in industrial water and process applications.

  • Consistent output for municipal transfer and booster stations
  • Heavy-duty plungers and valves for long service life
  • Configurable materials for abrasive or corrosive duty

Piston Pumps

Precision flow

Overview

Single- or double-acting piston designs that deliver accurate dosing and dependable pressure for treatment and process lines.

  • Ideal for chemical dosing and metered injection systems
  • Smooth controllability across varying head conditions
  • Compact footprints for skid and containerized plants

Reciprocating Pumps

Rugged duty

Overview

Reciprocating technology engineered for tough operating environments where reliability and repairability matter most.

  • Handles high-viscosity and solids-laden fluids
  • Field-serviceable components to reduce downtime
  • Proven performance in ETP, WTP, and utility networks

Deep cleaning

Booster Pumps

Pressure boosting systems that keep water moving reliably—from residential buildings to compact inline installs and intelligent VFD packages.

Residential

Domestic Booster Pumps

Domestic booster pumps maintain consistent outlet pressure across taps, showers, and appliances—especially in multi-floor buildings where supply pressure drops during peak use.

Hydromax packages combine quiet operation, dry-run protection, and easy integration with storage tanks so households and housing complexes get reliable water without frequent manual intervention.

Compact install

Inline Booster Pumps

Inline boosters install directly in the pipeline, saving floor space in mechanical rooms, rooftops, and utility closets where conventional pump skids will not fit.

They are suited for small commercial sites, clubhouses, and retrofit projects that need a pressure lift without major civil changes—delivering stable flow with minimal footprint and straightforward maintenance access.

Smart control

VFD Booster Systems

Variable-frequency drive booster systems match motor speed to real-time demand, reducing energy use and wear compared with fixed-speed operation during low-flow periods.

Ideal for campuses, hospitals, and industrial facilities with fluctuating consumption, VFD packages provide smooth pressure control, soft starts, and monitoring-ready interfaces for modern building management.

Centrifugal Pumps

Efficient rotodynamic pumps for transfer, circulation, and pressure boosting—engineered for stable hydraulic performance across municipal, commercial, and industrial networks.

01

End Suction Centrifugal Pumps

End suction centrifugal pumps are the workhorse of water distribution—easy to service, widely available, and well suited for clear water transfer in treatment plants, HVAC loops, and general industrial duty.

Hydromax selects impeller geometry, seal plans, and base-frame layouts for your head and flow curve so you get dependable operation with straightforward maintenance access.

02

Multistage Centrifugal Pumps

Multistage designs build pressure across several impellers in series, making them ideal for high-rise boosting, RO feed, boiler feed, and applications that need elevated head without oversized single-stage units.

We configure stage count, materials, and motor sizing to match your system curve—delivering smooth pressure rise, lower pulsation, and efficient performance through varying demand.

03

Self-Priming Pumps

Self-priming centrifugal pumps evacuate air from the suction line on startup, which simplifies installations where the pump sits above the water source or where intermittent suction conditions occur.

These units are commonly used in construction dewatering, emergency transfer, and sites with changing water levels—reducing manual priming steps and shortening time to stable operation.

04

Vertical Centrifugal Pumps

Vertical centrifugal pumps use a compact footprint with the motor mounted above the volute—excellent for sumps, pits, cooling towers, and installations where floor space is limited.

Hydromax vertical packages are built for reliable submersible or wet-pit duty with corrosion-resistant options, making them a strong fit for wastewater, stormwater, and process circulation systems.

Sludge Dewatering Pumps

Specialized pumping for thickened sludge, abrasive slurries, and chemical-conditioned dewatering lines—built for stable flow, wear resistance, and reliable uptime.

Progressive Cavity Pumps

Gentle, continuous flow for viscous sludge and polymer-conditioned streams—ideal for steady feed to belt presses, thickeners, and dewatering trains.

Slurry Pumps

Heavy-duty designs for abrasive solids and high-density slurries, with wear-resistant wet ends suited to tough municipal and industrial duty.

Diaphragm Pumps

Positive-displacement diaphragm units for precise, leak-resistant transfer of sludge, chemicals, and shear-sensitive dewatering feeds.
