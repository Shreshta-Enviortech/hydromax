Water Treatment

Treatment systems built for consistent, compliant water quality.

Filtration Systems

Hydromax designs and supplies filtration systems for municipal, industrial, and process water applications. From conventional media filters to advanced membrane technologies, each system is configured for your feed water quality, flow rate, and effluent standards.

Our filtration solutions remove suspended solids, turbidity, iron, manganese, and contaminants—delivering reliable treated water with minimal operational complexity.

Sand & Multi-Media Filters

Clarification

Overview

Gravity and pressure sand filters that remove suspended solids, turbidity, and particulates from raw water before downstream treatment or distribution.

  • Single, dual, and multimedia bed configurations
  • Automatic backwash for continuous operation
  • Suitable for municipal, industrial, and ETP applications

Activated Carbon Filters

Adsorption

Overview

Granular activated carbon beds designed to remove chlorine, odour, colour, organic compounds, and taste from process and potable water streams.

  • Effective pre-treatment for RO and UV systems
  • High surface area media for maximum adsorption
  • Reactivation and media replacement services available

Iron & Manganese Removal

Oxidation filtration

Overview

Pressure filtration systems combining oxidation and catalytic media to remove dissolved iron and manganese from borehole and groundwater sources.

  • Greensand and catalytic media options
  • Handles high iron concentrations typical of borehole water
  • Low chemical consumption with air oxidation options

Membrane Filtration (UF/MF)

Barrier filtration

Overview

Ultrafiltration and microfiltration membrane systems that provide a physical barrier against bacteria, viruses, colloids, and fine suspended solids.

  • Consistent effluent quality regardless of feed variations
  • Ideal pre-treatment for RO and nanofiltration systems
  • Automated CIP (clean-in-place) for minimal downtime

Disinfection Systems

Reliable pathogen elimination for potable water, reuse, and process applications—from chlorination to advanced UV and ozone treatment.

Chemical dosing

Chlorination Systems

Gas chlorination, sodium hypochlorite dosing, and on-site generation systems that provide dependable residual disinfection across distribution networks, storage tanks, and treatment plants.

Hydromax packages include dosing pumps, flow-paced controllers, residual analysers, and safety containment—designed for both continuous dosing and breakpoint chlorination applications.

Chemical-free

UV Disinfection

Ultraviolet disinfection reactors that inactivate bacteria, viruses, and protozoa without altering water chemistry—eliminating disinfection by-products and the need for chemical storage.

Our UV systems are available from compact point-of-use units to large-scale reactors for municipal plants, with dose monitoring and automated lamp management for consistent performance.

Advanced oxidation

Ozonation

Ozone generation and contact systems that provide powerful oxidation for disinfection, colour removal, taste and odour elimination, and micro-pollutant destruction in water and wastewater.

Ozonation integrates with biological treatment to enhance biodegradability of recalcitrant compounds, making it a strong choice for advanced treatment and water reuse schemes.

Softening & Conditioning

Ion exchange, reverse osmosis, and advanced demineralisation systems that condition water to the precise quality your process demands—from scale control to ultrapure production.

01

Ion Exchange Softeners

Ion exchange softeners replace calcium and magnesium ions with sodium, eliminating hardness that causes scale in boilers, heat exchangers, cooling systems, and process equipment.

Hydromax supplies single- and twin-vessel systems with automatic regeneration timers or conductivity-triggered cycles, matched to your water hardness and duty flow requirements.

02

Reverse Osmosis Systems

RO systems use semi-permeable membranes to remove dissolved salts, TDS, heavy metals, nitrates, and micro-contaminants—delivering high-purity permeate for drinking water, food processing, pharmaceuticals, and industrial use.

We design skid-mounted RO packages with pre-treatment, high-pressure pumping, membrane arrays, and post-treatment to match your feed water analysis and target permeate quality.

03

Demineralisation Plants

Mixed bed and two-bed demineralisation systems that reduce conductivity to ultrapure levels—essential for high-pressure boiler feed, semiconductor rinsing, and laboratory-grade water production.

Hydromax DM plants are engineered for reliable regeneration cycles with minimal acid and caustic consumption, delivering consistent water purity for sensitive process applications.

04

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)

ZLD systems combine evaporation, crystallisation, and advanced membrane processes to eliminate wastewater discharge—recovering water for reuse and concentrating residuals to solid by-products for responsible disposal.

Ideal for industries facing stringent discharge regulations or water scarcity, Hydromax ZLD solutions are engineered to maximise water recovery while minimising energy and operational costs.

Water Quality Management

Monitoring, control, and reporting systems that keep your treatment plant operating within compliance limits—with real-time data and automated responses.

Online Analysers

Continuous monitoring of pH, turbidity, chlorine residual, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, and other critical parameters with automated alarm and dosing response.

SCADA & Remote Control

Plant-wide supervisory control and data acquisition systems that centralise monitoring, automate sequences, and enable remote operation and data logging for compliance reporting.

Coagulation & Flocculation

Dosing and mixing systems for coagulants and flocculants that aggregate fine suspended solids before sedimentation or filtration, improving downstream treatment efficiency.
